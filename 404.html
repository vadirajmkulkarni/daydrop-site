Getting started

How many tasks can I add to Today?

Add as many tasks as you need. There is no three-task limit. Keep ideas in Later and move them to Today whenever you are ready.

Do I need an account or internet access?

No signup is needed for your daily plan or focus timer. Your data stays on your device unless you choose optional device backup. Backup, restore, support links, and any future purchases require a connection.

What happens to unfinished tasks tomorrow?

Your unfinished tasks are kept rather than silently deleted. Open the app to review your plan for the new day and decide what belongs in Today. The exact rollover behavior is explained in the app.

How do focus sessions work?

Choose a 5, 15, or 25 minute session and work on one small thing. Daydrop keeps the session’s end time when you leave the screen. It does not block other apps.

Can I move a cloud backup to a new phone?

The optional backup is tied to an anonymous device identity, with no email sign-in or cross-device recovery. It is intended for manual backup and restore with that same identity. A reinstall or reset can lose access. Keep your own copy of important tasks.

How do I delete my data?

In settings, Delete my data removes your optional cloud backup and anonymous identity, then clears local data. See the data deletion guide. If you need help, contact us privately rather than posting your identifier publicly.

Where can I buy or restore Daydrop Plus?

Purchases are not available in the current development version. Optional one-time Plus themes are planned. When they are enabled, the app will show the real purchase details and a Restore purchases control. There is no subscription offered by this development version.

Where do I download Daydrop?

Daydrop is currently being built for iPhone. It does not yet have a public App Store download or release date. This site provides product information and support policies.

Your privacy matters here, too.

Do not email passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive task contents. Read our privacy policy for how support messages are handled.
