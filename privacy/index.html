Privacy policy

Your day belongs to you.
Here is what happens to its data.

Effective date: October 17, 2026 · Developer: Medha Arcot

At a glance

Daydrop works without signing up. Tasks, focus sessions, wins, and preferences are saved on your device. Optional cloud backup sends a copy to Firebase only when you choose to back up. We do not use advertising or behavior analytics.

1. Who is responsible

Daydrop is provided by Medha Arcot (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Daydrop app and this support website. For privacy questions or requests, email tinyquotesapp@gmail.com with “Daydrop” in the subject.

2. Information on your device

The app stores the task text you enter, your Today and Later lists, completion and focus-session history, saved wins, and app preferences on your device. These are used to provide your plan, timer, and history. No name, email address, contact list, precise location, or advertising identifier is required.

Local data may also be included in operating-system backups according to your device and Apple account settings. We do not control those backups. Deleting Daydrop or erasing local app data can remove your local records; an operating-system backup may still retain a copy.

3. Optional device backup

Backup is optional and separate from the app’s offline features. When you use backup, Daydrop uses Firebase Authentication to create a random anonymous device identity and Cloud Firestore to save your app data, including task text, lists, focus history, wins, settings, and backup metadata. This identity does not require an email or password, but it is an identifier associated with your backup; it is not a promise of complete anonymity.

Backup and restore are manual actions. A backup is associated with the current device identity. It is not an account with cross-device sign-in. If that identity is lost after a reinstall, device reset, or deletion, we may be unable to locate or restore the old backup. Do not rely on this feature as your only copy of important information.

Google processes the backed-up data and technical connection information needed to provide and protect Firebase, such as IP addresses and user-agent information. Firebase Authentication documents short-term IP-address logging and its own deletion lifecycle. See Firebase’s privacy and security information and Google’s privacy policy.

4. Purchases

The current development version does not offer purchases. An optional Daydrop Plus one-time purchase for cosmetic themes is planned; the daily planning and focus features remain useful without it. If purchasing is enabled in a future release, the App Store will process payment and RevenueCat will help validate and restore access. That integration may process a separate app user identifier, transaction and receipt information, product identifiers, purchase status, and technical device information. We do not receive payment-card details. We will update this policy to reflect the released purchasing behavior before enabling it. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

5. Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use them to answer you and handle the request. Please avoid including private task contents, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information unless needed to resolve your request. Email is handled by Google’s Gmail service.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information, including your IP address and browser information, to serve and protect the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, remotely loaded fonts, or tracking pixels to this website. Following an external link opens a service with its own privacy practices.

6. How we use and share information

We use information to provide the features you choose, protect the service, respond to support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. Google, GitHub, Apple, and RevenueCat have the service roles described above; their handling of information is governed by their applicable terms and privacy commitments. We may disclose information if legally required or to protect rights and safety, subject to applicable law. The app does not publish your tasks to other users.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you remove it. The optional cloud backup is replaced when you back up again and remains until you delete it; no automatic expiry is currently promised. Removing a local task does not remove it from an existing cloud backup until you replace or delete that backup.

Use the app’s data controls to delete your cloud backup and anonymous identity while you still have access to the original device identity. The Delete my data action also erases local app data after the cloud deletion succeeds. Read the step-by-step deletion guide. The app’s deletion control does not erase operating-system backups. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete a cloud backup.

Service providers may retain limited records or copies for their documented security, backup, and legal needs. Google states that, after deletion is initiated, Firebase Authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days. Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary support records; you may ask us to delete it, subject to legal requirements.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use the core app without backup, choose not to contact support, delete data through the app, or contact us about access, correction, deletion, or other rights provided by the law where you live. We may need information to verify a request and identify the relevant device record. Because backup uses an anonymous device identity, an email address alone may not let us identify your backup. We respond as required by applicable law. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

9. Safeguards and international processing

We use the device’s app storage, Firebase authentication, and access controls for the intended device record. No storage or network service can guarantee absolute security. Optional cloud data and support messages may be processed in countries other than your own through the providers described in this policy and under their applicable safeguards. Daydrop does not offer end-to-end encrypted cloud backup.

10. Children and policy changes

Daydrop is a general productivity app, and we do not design data collection to identify children. If you believe a child has sent information that should be removed, contact us so we can address the request in accordance with applicable law.

We may revise this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected in the app or website as appropriate.
