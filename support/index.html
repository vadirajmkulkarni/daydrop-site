Getting started

How many tasks can I add to Today?

Add as many tasks as you need. There is no three-task limit. Keep ideas in Later and move them to Today whenever you are ready.

Do I need an account or internet access?

No signup is needed for your daily plan or focus timer. Your data stays on your device unless you choose optional device backup. Backup, restore, support links, and any future purchases require a connection.

What happens to unfinished tasks tomorrow?

Unfinished tasks move to Later when Daydrop starts a new day, so they stay safely out of today’s way. Move any of them back to Today when you’re ready. A task in an active focus session stays with that session until it finishes.

How do focus sessions work?

Choose a 5, 15, or 25 minute session and work on one small thing. In 1.1, you can start from any unfinished task. Pause or end the session when needed. Daydrop keeps its end time when you leave the screen, and records a completed session when you return. It does not block other apps or send a background timer alarm.

Can I move a cloud backup to a new phone?

The optional backup is tied to an anonymous device identity, with no email sign-in or cross-device recovery. It is intended for manual backup and restore with that same identity. A reinstall or reset can lose access. Keep your own copy of important tasks.

How do I delete my data?

In settings, Delete my data removes your optional cloud backup and anonymous identity, then clears local data. See the data deletion guide. If you need help, contact us privately rather than posting your identifier publicly.

Where can I buy or restore Daydrop Plus?

Purchases are not available in the current version. Optional one-time Plus themes are planned. When they are enabled, the app will show the real purchase details and a Restore purchases control. There is no subscription offered by the current version.

Where do I download Daydrop?

Find Daydrop on the App Store for iPhone. The refreshed 1.1 experience, including streaks and weekly progress, is coming soon. Availability may vary by storefront.

A little about streaks

COMING IN DAYDROP 1.1
What counts toward a streak?

A local calendar day is active when you complete at least one task or finish one 5, 15, or 25 minute focus session. Several tasks and sessions on the same day still count as one active day. A focus session counts even if you leave the task unfinished.

What happens if I miss a day?

A missed day ends the current streak, but your best streak and saved wins stay. If yesterday was active, your current streak remains open while you still have today to add a win. There are no streak purchases, freezes, or penalties.

Does my existing history count?

Yes. Progress is calculated from the task completions and finished focus sessions already saved in Daydrop. Your weekly view and totals use the same history, with no extra signup or upload.

Why did my streak change after undoing a task?

Progress reflects your current saved history. Undoing the only completion on a day can remove that active day. A finished focus session on that day still counts. The calculation uses the device’s local calendar, so changing its time zone can also change which day a recorded activity belongs to.
