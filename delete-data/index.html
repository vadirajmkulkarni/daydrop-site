A complete clean slate.

The app’s Delete my data control removes the optional cloud backup and guest identity first, then clears your local plan. Uninstalling Daydrop alone does not remove a previously saved cloud backup. Delete through the app while the original identity is still available.

Delete your Daydrop data

  1. Open Daydrop on the original device and open settings, titled A little housekeeping.
  2. If you want a copy first, choose Copy my data and paste it into a private file.
  3. Tap Delete my data near the bottom of settings.
  4. Read the Start with a clean slate? confirmation and choose Delete my data.
  5. If you have used cloud backup, keep an internet connection until deletion completes. When successful, settings closes and your app data is cleared.

The action removes the Daydrop cloud backup associated with your current guest identity, requests deletion of that Firebase anonymous identity, and then erases local tasks, wins, focus sessions, and preferences. If cloud deletion fails, the local reset is not performed: reconnect and try again, or contact support. A new backup later creates a new identity.

Cloud backup may be unavailable in some builds. If it was never used, the deletion control clears local data without creating a cloud identity. If you previously used backup in a different installation, follow the request process below.

Device and iCloud backups

Device or iCloud backups are controlled separately through Apple’s settings and may retain copies. Uninstalling the app can remove its local storage, but it does not request cloud deletion from Daydrop or erase Apple backups.

Lost the original device or identity?

Email tinyquotesapp@gmail.com with the subject “Daydrop data deletion”. Describe the request and include the Daydrop device identifier if you still have it. Do not post the identifier publicly or send passwords or sensitive task contents.

We may need to verify that the record belongs to you. Because there is no registered email or password for a backup, your email address alone may not identify it. If the original anonymous identity has been lost, we may be unable to find or verify a particular backup.

What deletion does and does not cover

Deleted app data cannot be restored through Daydrop. Service providers may keep limited security, legal, or backup records under their own policies; Google documents up to 180 days to remove deleted Firebase Authentication data from live and backup systems. Deleting Daydrop data does not erase Apple purchase records or support correspondence. You can separately request deletion of support messages by email, subject to applicable legal requirements.

The current version does not offer paid purchases. If purchasing is added later, refunds and store transaction records will remain managed by Apple. See our privacy policy for details.
